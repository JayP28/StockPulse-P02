Linear Regression Price Forecast

1 year of daily closing prices is fetched from Yahoo Finance. Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) linear regression is fitted to find the best-fit trend line, then projected 30 and 90 trading days forward. R² measures how well the straight line fits the historical data (0 = no fit, 1 = perfect fit).
